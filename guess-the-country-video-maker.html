    How it works

    Each question shows a country's outline with a countdown. On the reveal, the shape fills with the country's flag and the name appears. Shapes come from Natural Earth map data, simplified so they stay crisp on a phone.

    Make it your own

    • Levels ramp from easy shapes like Italy to extreme ones like Eswatini.
    • Choose a region for a Europe-only or Africa-only video.
    • Switch Answers to multiple choice to show four countries from the same part of the world.

    Questions people ask

    Which countries are included?

    162 countries with recognizable outlines. Tiny island nations are left out because their shapes are unreadable on a phone.

    How are borders drawn?

    Borders follow Natural Earth map data, and India, Pakistan and China are drawn as each country officially maps itself. You can swap out any country you prefer not to include.
