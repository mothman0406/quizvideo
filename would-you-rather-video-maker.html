    How it works

    Type one question per line as option A | option B. Each one becomes a split-screen card with a countdown and a prompt to comment, which is what gets replies on these videos.

    About percentages

    If you ran a real poll, add the results at the end of the line (| 62 | 38) and the video animates them on the answer screen. Leave them out otherwise; made-up numbers get called out in the comments.
