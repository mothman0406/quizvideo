    How it works

    Capital quizzes work because people guess the biggest city. In multiple choice, the wrong answers are each country's largest other cities, so Australia comes with Sydney, Melbourne and Perth, and Canada with Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal.

    Checked answers

    Every capital is checked against Wikidata and Natural Earth, and disagreements were reviewed by hand, including recent changes like Equatorial Guinea moving its capital to Ciudad de la Paz in 2026. Countries with more than one capital, like South Africa and Bolivia, show a short note on the answer screen.

    Questions people ask

    Can I show the answer without multiple choice?

    Yes. Set Answers to Reveal after the timer and the capital appears when the countdown ends.

    Can I make a Europe-only capitals quiz?

    Yes. Set Region to Europe, or any other continent.
