    Make a quiz video in about a minute

    1. Pick a format: Guess the Country, Guess the Flag, Guess the Capital, Would You Rather, or Trivia.
    2. Choose how many questions and how hard they get, or type your own.
    3. Press Download video for a 1080x1920 MP4, or Download slides for a photo carousel.

    Built for quiz accounts

    Every question gets a countdown, a reveal and sound effects, and the video ends by asking viewers for their score. Geography questions come from map data and capitals checked against two sources, sorted from easy to extreme so each video gets harder as it goes.

    Questions people ask

    Is it free?

    Yes. Every format and unlimited exports are free, with a small "made with quizvideo.app" mark. Pro removes it for $9 a month, $59 a year, or $49 once during the launch.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Open the page and start making videos.

    Where do my videos go?

    Straight to your device. The video is rendered and encoded in your browser, so nothing is uploaded to us.

    Which browsers work?

    Chrome and Edge on a computer, and Safari on iPhone. If a browser cannot encode sound, the video still exports and you can add music in the app you post from.

    How long are the videos?

    A 10-question video runs about 76 seconds. You can choose 5 to 15 questions and 3 to 10 seconds per question.

    Are the answers accurate?

    Capitals are checked against Wikidata and Natural Earth and were updated for 2026, including Equatorial Guinea moving its capital to Ciudad de la Paz.
