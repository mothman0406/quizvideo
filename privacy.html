Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026.

The short version

QuizVideo runs in your browser. The questions you type and the videos and slides you make are created on your device and are not sent to us. There are no accounts, no advertising trackers, and no tracking cookies.

What our host sees

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Payments

Pro purchases are handled by Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record and collects what it needs to process payment and taxes. We receive your email address and order details so we can provide your license and support. When you enter a license key, the app checks it with Polar.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies or personal identifiers. We also count how many videos and slide sets are exported each day. These are totals only and cannot identify you.

Children

QuizVideo is not directed to children under 13, and purchases are for adults 18 and older.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: support@quizvideo.app.
