How to make quiz videos for TikTok

You can make a TikTok quiz video in a few minutes, without designing frames in Canva or timing countdowns in CapCut. Here is the whole process.

1. Pick a format people answer in the comments

Guess the Country by shape, Guess the Flag, Guess the Capital, Would You Rather and Trivia all invite viewers to keep score and comment.

2. Set the length

Ten questions at five seconds each, plus a two-second reveal, runs about 76 seconds. That clears the one-minute minimum TikTok sets for Creator Rewards.

3. Ramp the difficulty

Open with easy questions so people stay, and end on extreme ones so they comment. The Easy to extreme setting does this for you.

4. Style it

Pick a theme or press Shuffle style, and set your own title. Changing the look between posts keeps your account from looking repetitive.

5. Export

Press Download video for a 1080x1920 MP4. It renders on your device, so nothing is uploaded.

6. Post it

Upload the MP4 to TikTok, add a trending sound, and write a caption that asks viewers how many they got.

Doing it by hand

In Canva or CapCut you would design a frame per question, time each countdown and reveal, and repeat it for every question, which can take an hour or more per video.
